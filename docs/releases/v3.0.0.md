**AETHER 3** mixes songs the way a DJ would, plays on your TV, brings your playlists in from anywhere, and moves more smoothly than ever.

### What's new
- **Automix,** in Settings under Sound: every song is measured on the phone (tempo, beats and bars, phrases, key, where the voice is) and run into the next in time, on the bar, the bass handed over, with a filter or an echo where it suits; a crossfade where two songs can't be mixed. The dial marks where the mix will be, and the cover turns into the next song's in a cloud of its own coloured dots for exactly as long as the mix lasts.
- **Play on a TV:** the cast key beside the video key on Now Playing finds the TVs and speakers with Google Cast on your Wi-Fi. The phone stays the source and the queue stays yours, its volume keys set the TV's volume, and "This phone" brings the music back where the TV had got to. As the music leaves, the screen is drawn up after it and a wave runs down through it. For now the TV shows Google's own player screen; AETHER's own TV screen is on its way.
- **Playlists in and out:** bring playlists in from a file (AETHER's own, M3U, CSV such as Exportify's, a list of songs, or Spotify's data download with every playlist and your liked songs) or from a public Spotify or Apple Music playlist's link. Each song is found on YouTube Music as exactly as AETHER can, and nothing is doubled. Export any playlist as an AETHER file, M3U8 or CSV, or all of them at once from Settings.
- **A sleep timer** in Now Playing's menu: after this song, 15, 30 or 45 minutes, an hour, or any length up to twelve hours. With a crossfade or Automix on, the song fades out at its end instead of running into the next.
- **The music keeps going:** ten songs suggested by the one playing wait after yours, topped up as each song begins, with the app closed too.
- **More covers that move:** for songs Apple Music has no film for, the community's index of looping covers. The index is fetched whole and matched on the phone; nothing about what you play is sent.
- **Explicit songs are marked** with a small grey E, as the catalogues mark them.

### Better
- **Now Playing grows out of the mini player** the way the Dynamic Island does, on Apple's springs, and opens and closes without building itself first.
- **Pages move with your thumb:** Android's back gesture slides AETHER's pages as swiping back does on iPhone, Back closes what's on top first, a folder flies home into wherever its place is now, and a page opened while another is still going home comes in over it.
- **The Library scrolls more smoothly,** the first time through most of all.
- **Search lists songs first,** five and then more, and every song shows its length.
- **An album filed by its music videos** (KR$NA's "Yours Truly", say) plays as its songs.
- **A music video plays without lyrics:** its sound runs to its own clock, so words timed to the song drifted from it.
- **Up Next moves songs as BitChord's does:** the song stays under your finger and the others slide aside to show where it will land.
- **Now Playing and the lyrics pull down** from the middle of the screen, and names of pages always fit their place.

### Fixed
- **Wrong lyrics:** one library's answers are now held to the song (it gave Tchaikovsky for a song called "Espresso"), and writers' names at the head of a file no longer show as sung lines.
- **Closing Now Playing** no longer flickers as it lands in the pill.
- **Automix** no longer crackles as the next song comes in, and a song on repeat no longer mixes into itself.
- **AETHER could close by itself** when one of your own sources was slow to answer. Sources and lyrics are now always read off the main thread.
- **Two folders touched at once** no longer both open.

### Installing
Needs Android 10 or later. AETHER 3.0 installs over any earlier AETHER and keeps your library; AETHER on your phone offers it by itself. Playing on a TV needs the phone and the TV on the same Wi-Fi.
