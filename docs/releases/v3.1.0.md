**AETHER 3.1** keeps your playlists in step with YouTube Music, puts the song playing on your home screen, lights Now Playing in the cover's own colours, and keeps songs in seconds.

### What's new
- **Sync with YouTube Music:** a key on every playlist, on Liked songs, and on your own YouTube Music playlists' pages. On, a playlist and its copy in your account are kept as one, both ways: a song added or taken out on either side is added or taken out on the other, and so is a new name. A playlist made in AETHER is made in your account too, private; one of your account's becomes one of AETHER's; Liked songs follow your account's liked songs. Its arrows turn while it syncs. Off, it stays as it last was, and deleting a playlist in AETHER leaves its copy in your account.
- **Home-screen widgets:** 2×2 and 4×2, the song playing with its cover going out to black in rings round its keys, as Now Playing's film goes out round the dial, and Now Playing's keys. The play key turns the moment it's touched, and a tap anywhere else opens Now Playing.
- **Light from the cover:** behind Now Playing, four soft lights in the cover's own colours, slowly orbiting while the song plays, a new song's light fading in over the last. Turn it off in Settings and Now Playing is black, as before.
- **Put any list in order:** the Library's playlists, Kept, the phone's music, your songs, albums and artists, and every playlist. A key under the list names the order it's in: alphabetical, A to Z or Z to A; date added or date modified, newest first; and for songs their artist, album or length. Each list keeps its own.
- **Truer sound:** the equaliser and Automix now shape FLAC and other hi-res files too; Apple Lossless (ALAC) plays on every phone; ReplayGain (Settings, Sound) brings each song to one loudness by its own tags; and with a USB DAC on Android 14 or later, Bit-perfect sends lossless songs to it untouched, at their own sample rate and bit depth.
- **Latin letters first,** in Verse: the Latin letters sung as the line, large, and the words in their own script beneath them.
- **Words on the cover** can be turned off (Settings, Now Playing), leaving the picture alone.

### Fixed
- **Keeping songs takes seconds:** YouTube sends a song asked for in one go at about the speed it plays, so keeping one took minutes. Songs now come in short ranges, each at your connection's full speed, as BitChord's do, and the song playing buffers ahead faster too.
- **FLAC on Samsung phones** played as loud, fast static (#15, #16): Samsung's own decoder garbled it. FLAC now goes to Android's own decoder, BitChord's fix.
- **A song just kept** is at the top of Kept, not out of sight at its foot.
- **An artist's music videos** play one after another.
- **Ending a cast** brings the music back to the headphones or speaker it came from, not the phone's own speaker.
- **The fade round the dial** has no bands, and as the music goes out to a TV the screen is drawn up after it from edge to edge.

### Better
- **Now Playing's cover** is shown whole, square as it was made; the prints stay 4:5.
- **On the phone's own speaker** songs go out as 16-bit; through a DAC or headphones that take it, as float, every bit of a 24-bit file kept.

### Not yet tried
- The FLAC fix has been tried on a phone that isn't a Samsung, and Bit-perfect not yet with a USB DAC. Liked songs' sync has been checked a song at a time, not yet on a whole library. If any of them misbehaves for you, say so on #15.

### Installing
Needs Android 10 or later. AETHER 3.1 installs over any earlier AETHER and keeps your library; AETHER on your phone offers it by itself. Syncing needs you signed in to YouTube Music in AETHER.
